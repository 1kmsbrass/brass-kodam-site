HOW TO UPLOAD REAL PHOTOS:

1. Go to YOUR-SITE.pages.dev/admin/
2. Open the product or blog post
3. Click "Choose different image" or "+ Add item"
4. Click "Upload"
5. Select a photo from your computer
6. Give it a good filename: brass-kodam-no1-front.jpg
7. Add alt text: "Brass Kodam No.1 front view"
8. Click "Publish"

That's it! The old placeholder goes away, your photo appears.
