## Laser Engraving & Printing

We offer laser engraving/printing directly on brass and copper kodam — commonly used for:

- Temple name and consecration date, for Kumbabhishekam commemorative sets
- Deity name or Om/Aum and sacred symbols
- Donor or sponsor name, for temple donation-recognition pieces
- Corporate or event branding, for bulk gifting orders

Engraving is done after casting and polishing, so text stays sharp and doesn't wear off like paint. Share your text/logo requirement along with your kodam order and we'll quote engraving as part of the bulk price.
