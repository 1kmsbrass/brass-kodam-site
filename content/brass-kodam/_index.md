# Brass Kodam — Pithalai Kodam Guide & Wholesale

A brass kodam (பித்தளை குடம் / பித்தளை கோடம்) is a traditional brass vessel used for temple ceremonies, Kumbabhishekam, pooja, religious functions and other ceremonial purposes. This page is the main guide to brass kodam: what it is, the names people use for it, sizes and capacity, common uses, brass quality, and wholesale purchasing.

## What is a Brass Kodam?

A **brass kodam** is a vessel made from brass, traditionally shaped with a rounded body and narrower neck. Depending on region and usage, the same or similar vessel may be called a **brass kudam, pithalai kodam, pithalai kudam, brass kumbam or brass kalasam**.

The exact shape, proportions, finish and terminology can vary between manufacturers, regions and ritual applications. For a bulk purchase, it is therefore better to specify the intended use, approximate size, capacity, dimensions and required finish rather than relying only on a product name.

## Brass Kodam for Kumbabhishekam

The kumbam or sacred vessel has a central role in Kumbabhishekam. The word **Kumbabhishekam** combines *kumbam*, referring to the sacred pot or vessel, and *abhishekam*, referring to ritual bathing or pouring sanctified substances.

Brass kodams are therefore closely associated with temple consecration ceremonies and are commonly required in quantity for Kumbabhishekam arrangements. The number, size, preparation and exact ritual use of vessels depend on the temple, tradition and Agamic procedures followed. Ritual requirements should be confirmed with the temple's priests or ceremony organizers.

## Brass Kodam Sizes, Capacity & Weight

There is no single universal sizing standard across every brass manufacturer. A useful specification should include:

- Kodam number or model name
- Approximate height and maximum width
- Water capacity
- Approximate weight
- Neck and opening dimensions
- Plain, polished or engraved finish

For wholesale buying, **weight is especially important** because brass products are commonly purchased and quoted according to metal weight, workmanship and quantity. Two vessels with similar external dimensions can have different weights because of differences in sheet thickness and construction.

## Why Brass is Used

Brass combines copper and zinc and has long been used for traditional Indian vessels and ritual objects. Buyers generally choose brass because it offers a distinctive golden appearance, durability and suitability for making a wide range of vessel shapes.

For ceremonial purchases, appearance is only one consideration. **Construction quality, wall thickness, weight consistency, finish and dimensional consistency** matter when ordering multiple pieces.

### What to check before a bulk order

1. Confirm the exact model or sample.
2. Confirm approximate weight per piece.

4. Confirm whether the quoted price includes polishing or other finishing.
5. Confirm engraving requirements, if any.
6. Confirm quantity and packing requirements.
7. Request a sample or production reference when consistency is important.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a brass kodam?

A brass kodam is a traditional brass vessel used for ceremonial, pooja, temple and other purposes. Depending on region and context, it may also be called a brass kudam or brass kumbam.

### Is brass kodam used for Kumbabhishekam?

Yes. Brass vessels are commonly associated with Kumbabhishekam and other temple ceremonies. The exact number, size and ritual preparation should be confirmed with the temple priests or ceremony organizers.

### What is pithalai kodam?

**Pithalai kodam (பித்தளை குடம்)** is a Tamil term for a brass kodam. Pithalai means brass, while kodam refers to a pot or vessel.

### How do I choose the right brass kodam size?

Start with the intended use and required capacity, then compare height, width, opening, weight and construction. For a bulk order, confirm the exact specification using a sample or agreed dimensions.

### Is brass kodam sold wholesale?

Yes. Brass kodams can be purchased in bulk for temples, Kumbabhishekam events, pooja retailers, distributors and other institutional requirements. Wholesale pricing depends on specification, weight, quantity, finish and other order requirements.

### Why can two brass kodams of the same size have different prices?

The external size alone does not determine the amount of brass used. Sheet thickness, finished weight, workmanship, design, polishing and other finishing work can change the cost significantly.
