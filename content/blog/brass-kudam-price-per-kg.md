# Brass Kudam Price Per Kg: How to Compare Quotes Properly

If you buy brass products in quantity, sooner or later somebody will quote you a **price per kg**.

That can be useful.

It can also create confusion.

The important thing is to understand what the number actually represents.

## A finished kudam is not just a pile of brass

The final price of a finished brass kudam can include several things:

- Brass material
- Manufacturing
- Labour
- Wastage or process loss
- Finishing and polishing
- Packing
- Supplier margin
- Transport
- GST, depending on how the quotation is presented

So a finished-product rate per kg should not automatically be treated as the same thing as the day's raw brass market rate.

Those are different numbers.

## Why actual weight matters

Suppose a supplier says:

**“This is ₹X per kg.”**

Your next question should be:

**“What is the approximate weight of one finished piece?”**

Without the weight, the per-kg quote doesn't tell you the actual piece cost.

For a bulk order, ask for:

**Approximate weight per piece × quantity × quoted finished-product rate**

Then separately confirm GST, packing and transport.

## Per kg versus per piece

Both can be useful.

### Piece price

Easy for most customers to understand.

> ₹___ per kudam

### Weight-based comparison

Useful for wholesale buyers comparing similar products.

> ₹___ per kg, with each kudam approximately ___ kg

The second format gives you more information, but only if the product specifications are comparable.

## Don't compare different kudams only by kg

This is a common mistake.

A more expensive-looking product may have different dimensions, a different shape or different workmanship.

So the comparison should be:

**Same product → same size → similar specifications → weight → rate**

Otherwise you may be comparing apples with brass coconuts.

## Ask whether the quote includes GST and transport

A supplier can quote a low number and still have a higher delivered cost.

For a serious purchase, ask:

- Is GST included?
- Is packing included?
- Is transport included?
- Is the rate for one piece or for the full quantity?
- How long is the quotation valid?

Metal-related costs can change, so an old quotation should not be treated as today's rate.

## For wholesale buyers

Send a complete requirement:

> Brass kudam  
> Size: ___  
> Approximate quantity: ___ pieces  
> Delivery location: ___

Then ask for the current rate and approximate weight.

That is enough to turn a vague price enquiry into a real quotation request.

## One practical rule

**Never compare a brass kudam quote by price per kg alone.**

Compare the actual product, its dimensions, weight and final delivered cost.

That is how you avoid getting excited by a cheap number that belongs to a different product.
