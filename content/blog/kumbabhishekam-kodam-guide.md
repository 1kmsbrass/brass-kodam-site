Choosing the right kodam for a Kumbabhishekam depends on three factors: metal, size, and quantity required by your ritual scale (108 / 1008 / custom).

### Brass vs Copper
Brass kodam are the most common choice for cost-effective bulk Kumbabhishekam orders. Copper kodam are preferred where the ritual specifically calls for pure copper vessels.

### Sizing
Standard sizes are matched to the gopuram/vimana count being consecrated — talk to your temple's Vastu/Agama consultant, then contact us for the matching wholesale set.
