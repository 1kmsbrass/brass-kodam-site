# What Should I Check Before Buying a Brass Kudam Online?

Buying a brass kudam online is convenient.

It is also a good way to discover that a photograph does not have a weighing scale attached to it.

That is why the sensible approach is simple:

**Use the internet to find the product. Use questions to verify the product.**

## Don't judge brass only from the photograph

A photograph can show:

- Shape
- Design
- Finish
- General appearance

It cannot reliably show:

- Actual weight
- Exact dimensions
- Capacity
- Brass thickness
- How the item is packed
- What you will actually receive

So before ordering, ask for the basic specifications.

## The five questions I would ask first

1. **What is the height?**
2. **What is the diameter?**
3. **What is the approximate weight?**
4. **What is the capacity, if relevant?**
5. **Is this the actual product photograph?**

If the seller can answer these clearly, you have something concrete to compare.

## Ask for real photographs

If you are buying a substantial or bulk order, ask for photographs of the actual products where possible.

Useful photographs can include:

- Product from different angles
- Multiple pieces together
- Product being weighed
- Packing before dispatch
- Actual finished stock

For example, real order photographs can show that products are actually being handled, checked and dispatched.

## Be careful with very cheap prices

A very low price is not automatically a scam.

But it is a reason to ask another question:

**“What exactly am I getting?”**

Perhaps the product is lighter.

Perhaps the dimensions are smaller.

Perhaps GST or delivery is extra.

Perhaps the price applies only to a particular quantity.

Perhaps the photograph shows a different model.

Cheap can be perfectly legitimate. You just need to know what the number describes.

## Check packing and damage policy

Brass is durable.

A parcel containing several brass vessels is also heavy.

Ask how the items will be packed and what happens if something is damaged in transit.

For a larger order, this matters much more than it does for a small household purchase.

## Check the final payable amount

Before paying, confirm:

- Product price
- Quantity
- GST
- Packing charges
- Delivery charges
- Total payable amount
- Expected dispatch time
- Damage/replacement process

If you are buying for a shop or temple, also ask for the invoice requirements you need.

## Online does not mean “no conversation”

A good online purchase should still allow you to ask questions.

Send the seller a photo or product link and say:

> “I need ___ pieces. Please confirm the size, approximate weight, current rate and delivery to ___.”

That is a perfectly normal wholesale enquiry.

## For ritual purchases

If the kudam is for a pooja, Kumbabhishekam or temple ceremony, confirm the required size and quantity with the priest, temple authority or organiser.

Don't let an online photograph decide a ritual specification.

## The practical rule

Before buying a brass kudam online, know **what it weighs, how big it is, what it costs delivered, and what happens if it arrives damaged.**

Once those four things are clear, online buying becomes much less mysterious.
