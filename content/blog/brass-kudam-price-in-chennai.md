# What Is the Brass Kudam Price in Chennai?

If you are searching for **brass kudam price in Chennai**, you probably want one thing:

**“Tell me how much it will actually cost me.”**

Fair enough.

The difficulty is that the shop price is only part of the calculation.

If the kudam has to travel to Chennai, the useful number is the **delivered cost for the exact product and quantity you need**.

## Start with the product

Before comparing prices, identify the product properly.

Ask for:

- Height
- Maximum diameter
- Opening diameter
- Approximate weight
- Capacity, if relevant
- Finish
- Quantity

If you compare one seller's large, heavy kudam with another seller's lighter version, the cheaper number doesn't necessarily mean the cheaper deal.

## Chennai price versus delivered price

Suppose one supplier is nearby and another supplier is outside Chennai.

The outside supplier may have a lower product price but higher transport and packing costs.

Or the opposite may happen.

So for a bulk buyer, compare:

**Product price + GST + packing + transport = delivered cost**

Ask each supplier to explain what is included.

That gives you a much cleaner comparison.

## Quantity changes the conversation

Buying one kudam is one thing.

Buying 25, 50 or 100 pieces is another.

If you need multiple pieces, tell the supplier the quantity when requesting the quote.

A useful enquiry looks like this:

> “I need ___ brass kudams of approximately ___ size for delivery to Chennai. Please send the current rate, approximate weight and delivered charges.”

That is much easier to quote accurately.

## What about “price in Chennai” search results?

Online prices can be useful for getting a rough idea of the market.

But don't assume every displayed price represents the same product.

Check:

- Product size
- Weight
- Quantity
- GST
- Delivery
- Return or damage policy
- Whether the photograph represents the exact product

The shiny photograph is not the specification.

## For temples and Kumbabhishekam

If the kudam is being purchased for a temple ceremony or Kumbabhishekam, confirm the required specification with the priest, temple authority or organiser before placing the order.

The correct size is more important than simply finding the biggest or cheapest vessel.

Ritual requirements can vary by temple and tradition.

## A better way to ask for a Chennai quote

Send three things:

**1. Product or photo**  
**2. Quantity**  
**3. Chennai delivery location**

If you know the required size, include that too.

Then ask for the current quotation and approximate weight per piece.

Now you are comparing actual products instead of search-result numbers.
