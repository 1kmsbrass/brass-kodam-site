When a temple trust or event contractor starts planning a Kumbabhishekam, one of the first things needed is a bulk order of kodam (kalasam) — often 108 or 1008 pieces for larger ceremonies.

## Step 1: Decide on Size

The kodam size depends on your ceremony scale and the number of gopuram/temple spires being consecrated. See our [Size Guide](/products/size-guide/) for No.0–No.6 options. Most Kumbabhishekam orders use No.1–No.2 for handling ease, or No.4–No.6 for main sanctum / gopuram placement.

## Step 2: Choose Metal

**Brass kodam** — most cost-effective, standard for Kumbabhishekam. Bright, durable finish.  
**Copper kodam** — preferred where ritual specifically calls for pure copper. Higher cost.

See our [Products](/products/) for detailed comparisons.
